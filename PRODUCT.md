## Users

Primary audience: software engineering recruiters, hiring managers, technical interviewers, and potential collaborators.

They arrive to judge whether Arushi Shree Manoj is a credible engineering candidate or collaborator. Their job is to understand engineering capability, professional experience, technical interests, and selected projects quickly enough to decide next: explore work, review skills and experience, open the resume, or follow professional profiles.

Career and engineering reading must stay prominent. Visual personality is secondary to that job.

## Product Purpose

A personal engineering portfolio for Arushi Shree Manoj: Computer Science undergraduate at IGDTUW, graduating May 2027; full-stack developer and aspiring software engineer.

Success is an interactive introduction to the person and engineer behind the work, not a restyled resume or a list of repositories. Visitors should leave able to:

- see how she approaches problems and makes engineering decisions
- distinguish her actual contributions and owned responsibilities from collaborative work
- follow progression across full-stack development, data automation, machine learning, and computer vision
- enter interactive case studies that cover thought process, challenges, and technical decisions
- reach resume and professional profiles without hunting
